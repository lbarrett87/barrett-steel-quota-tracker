Frequently asked questions

What is a steel safeguard / trade measure quota?

It's a government-set limit on how much steel can be imported tariff-free from a given country within a defined period. Once the quota balance for that category and origin is used up, further imports face a 25% tariff until the next period opens.

What happens when a quota is exhausted?

Imports in that category and origin still go ahead, but incur the out-of-quota tariff rate until the next quota period begins.

How often is this data updated?

Automatically, once a day, direct from the UK Government's Trade Tariff service. You can also trigger a manual refresh using the button above.
